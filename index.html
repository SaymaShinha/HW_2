Vision

To be the center of excellence for quality education, research and innovation.

History

Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology, Gazipur (abbreviated as DUET) was founded as the College of Engineering, Dhaka with the motto “Technology is the Advancement” at its temporary campus at Tejgaon, Dhaka, Bangladesh in 1980. It was then renamed as Dhaka Engineering College (DEC) under the University of Dhaka in 1981, and used to offer Bachelor of Engineering programs in Civil, Electrical, and Mechanical disciplines.

Mision

  • To provide a congenial environment for world-class education, research and innovation.
  • To produce highly efficient technical professionals endowed with practical knowledge, skills and ethical values based on emerging demands.
